Clinical Insights & Recommendations — Jan–Jun 2026 (6 Months Complete): Analysis covers Medical Imaging (MID) and Laboratory performance for January to June 2026 vs the same period 2025 (lab May 2025 not in source). June closed H1 as the strongest month of 2026 for both divisions — MID 12,635 studies (+13.2% vs May) and Lab 223,944 tests (+11.0% vs May). Important: June 2025 was an anomalously low baseline for both divisions (MID 6,654; Lab 116,402), so the very large June year over year figures are inflated. The clean signal is the month over month step up and the record absolute volumes.

🟢 MID — Growth Drivers (Jan–Jun)

  • 🥇MRI — Record month in June (1,691): Jan 1,419 → … → May 1,524 → Jun 1,691. 6-month total 8,609 vs 6,528 (+31.9% YoY) — the clear MID growth engine, up every month above 2025.
  • 🥈CT — Record month in June (1,479): 6-month 7,814 vs 6,773 (+15.4% YoY). Consistent upward trajectory.
  • 📊Ultrasound — Firmly positive over H1: June record 4,096; 6-month 20,620 vs 18,600 (+10.9% YoY). The earlier deficit is fully closed.
  • 🏅IR-CathLab — Structural step-up: 6-month 259 vs 101 (+156% YoY); June 56. Elevated baseline sustained across all six months.
  • 🏥Science Park MID — Record June (2,121): Feb 1,021 → … → May 1,706 → Jun 2,121, roughly 17% of total MID. Satellite imaging is the fastest scaling site (SP MRI, CT, US and X-Ray all set June highs).

🔴 MID — Areas Needing Action (Jan–Jun)

  • ⚠️DEXA — largest percentage decline: 6-month 1,251 vs 1,473 (−15.1%). June recovered to 235 but H1 remains well below 2025. Endocrinology and Orthopaedic referral pathways need sustained attention.
  • ⚠️Fluoroscopy — persistent decline: 6-month 940 vs 1,071 (−12.2%). Confirm how much is explained by the known scope change before treating as true demand loss.
  • ⚠️Nuclear Medicine soft: 6-month 670 vs 726 (−7.7%). June recovered to 132, but the modality is running below prior year. Monitor referral sources.
  • 📉Base-effect caution on June: X-Ray now reads +2.6% YTD, but this flip is driven entirely by the depressed June 2025 baseline (Jan–May was −7.3%). Treat June year over year gains across all modalities as inflated until July confirms the run rate.

🟢 Laboratory — Growth & Strengths (Jan–Jun)

  • 🥇June — record lab month of 2026 (223,944): +22,123 (+11.0%) above May, overtaking the April peak. Broad based across sections.
  • 🧪Chemistry — record June (171,506): +11.4% vs May. 6-month total 880,406 — 76.2% of all lab volume.
  • 📊6-Month Lab Total: 1,155,937 tests. A record first half. Volume has stepped up to a higher plateau through Q2.
  • 📈June records across sections: Hematology 12,042, Endocrinology 11,280 and Immunology 1,653 all set 2026 highs — the June strength is genuinely broad, not a single section spike.
  • 🧬Molecular rebound: June 2,165 is the strongest month since January, recovering the Q1 dip. Micro also set a June high at 3,908.

🔴 Laboratory — Concerns & Watch Items (Jan–Jun)

  • ✅Molecular recovering: Jan 2,444 → Mar 1,295 (trough) → Apr 1,474 → May 1,752 → Jun 2,165. The Q1 dip has largely closed, but keep MSR_2110_02146 (Molecular Technologist) moving to protect the recovery.
  • ⚠️POC still below its 2025 run rate: Jun 9,442 continues the climb from the March trough (6,348), yet remains under the equivalent 2025 months. Review ER and urgent care utilisation.
  • 📉Send Out dipped in June: Jun 1,744 vs May 1,838 is the only major section down month over month. Confirm whether insourcing is shifting volume in house (a positive) rather than lost demand.
  • 📉Blood Bank flat to soft: Jun 1,247 recovered from the May low (1,144) but the section is not growing with the rest of the lab. Watch inpatient surgical and transfusion ordering.
  • 🔔Molecular critical value reporting below benchmark: June 82.5% within 60 min vs 96% benchmark (94 of 114), the second month this year under target. All other quality indicators held, with GenLab critical values at 97.0%, Routine TAT 96.2%, STAT TAT 91.8% and specimen rejection at 0.55% well inside the 1.24% limit. Review Molecular notification workflow.

🎯 Top 6 Recommended Actions — Based on Jan–Jun 2026 Complete Data

  • 1️⃣Protect & Grow MRI Momentum: MRI set a record in June (1,691) and leads MID at +31.9% YTD. Assess additional slot capacity, extended hours, and protocol optimisation so MRI stays the strategic imaging growth driver through H2 2026.
  • 2️⃣Confirm the June Step Up is Sustainable: Both divisions set records, but June year over year is flattered by a very low June 2025 base. Track July closely to confirm the elevated run rate (MID ~12.6K, Lab ~224K) holds before committing capacity on it.
  • 3️⃣Validate DEXA & Fluoroscopy Declines: DEXA −15.1% and Fluoroscopy −12.2% YTD. Confirm how much Fluoroscopy is explained by the known scope change, and review Endocrine and Ortho pathways driving DEXA.
  • 4️⃣Scale Science Park: SP MID hit a record 2,121 in June (~17% of MID), with SP MRI, CT, US and X-Ray all rising. Evaluate staffing, equipment and scheduling headroom to capture the fastest growing site.
  • 5️⃣Lock In Molecular & POC Recovery: Both rebounded strongly in June. Keep MSR_2110_02146 (Molecular Technologist) moving and review ER and urgent care POC utilisation so the recovery holds.
  • 6️⃣Chemistry Capacity Planning: Chemistry set a June record (171,506) and is 76% of lab volume. Confirm analyser throughput, reagent supply and staffing headroom for sustained months above 170K.

📈 Multi-Year Growth Context — AHD Diagnostic Center

  • 📌2023 Imaging: 85,766 studies — Baseline year.
  • 📌2024 Imaging: 106,738 studies (+24.4%) — Exceptional expansion. MRI +28.7%, CT +19.8%, US +25.0%.
  • 📌2025 Imaging: 115,833 studies (+8.5%) — Sustainable growth. IR accelerated (+30.7%), PET-CT +17.1%.
  • 📌Jan–Jun 2026 Imaging: 65,544 studies (+9.0% vs Jan–Jun 2025 at 60,139): June set a record 12,635, though the year over year gain is amplified by a low June 2025 base. On the cleaner Jan–May view imaging was roughly flat; MRI (+31.9% YTD) and CT (+15.4%) lead, while DEXA and Fluoroscopy lag. Q2 closed at 35,273 (+18.1% vs Q2 2025).
  • 📌Jan–Jun 2026 Lab: 1,155,937 tests: A record first half. June (223,944) is the strongest month of 2026 and Chemistry set a June record (171,506). Growth is broad based across sections; June 2025 was a low comparator, so year over year is inflated.
  • 🔑Key Takeaway: Both divisions closed H1 at record June volumes and a higher Q2 plateau. Because June 2025 was an unusually low baseline, the headline year over year figures overstate growth: read June on a month over month basis and confirm the run rate in July. Priority: sustain MRI and Chemistry, scale Science Park, validate the DEXA and Fluoroscopy declines, and lock in the Molecular and POC recovery.
