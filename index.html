Clinical Insights & Recommendations — Jan–May 2026 (5 Months Complete): Analysis covers Medical Imaging (MID) and Laboratory performance for January to May 2026 vs the same period 2025 (lab May 2025 not in source). April was the peak month for both divisions; May normalised — MID at 11,163 studies (−5.7% vs May 2025) and Lab at 201,821 tests (second strongest of 2026, −3.3% vs April). April per modality MID figures were reconciled to the official statistics deck, which materially changed several year over year conclusions (notably Ultrasound and X-Ray).

🟢 MID — Growth Drivers (Jan–May)

  • 🥇MRI — Record month in May (1,524): Jan 1,419 → Feb 1,334 → Mar 1,132 → Apr 1,509 → May 1,524. May +20% vs May 2025 (1,268). 5-month total 6,918 vs 5,759 (+20.1% YoY) — the clear MID growth engine.
  • 🥈CT — Record month in May (1,437): +19% vs May 2025 (1,206). 5-month 6,335 vs 5,901 (+7.4% YoY). Consistent upward trajectory.
  • 🏅IR-CathLab — Structural step-up: 5-month 203 vs 87 (+133% YoY); May 41 vs 18 (+128%). New elevated baseline confirmed across all five months.
  • 📊Ultrasound — Now broadly flat YoY (corrected): With reconciled April actuals, 5-month US is 16,524 vs 16,618 (−0.6%). April (3,782) slightly exceeded Apr 2025; May (3,514) softened −7.9%. The earlier large deficit was an artefact of stale April data.
  • 🏥Science Park MID sustained: May 1,706 (vs Apr 1,696, +0.6%) — roughly 15% of total MID. Satellite imaging continues to hold its elevated April level.

🔴 MID — Areas Needing Action (Jan–May)

  • ⚠️X-Ray is the largest absolute YoY gap: 5-month 18,291 vs 19,737 (−7.3%, a deficit of 1,446 studies); May 3,733 vs 4,198 (−11.1%). As the highest volume modality (~35% of MID), this is the single biggest volume risk. Referral and scheduling accessibility review needed.
  • ⚠️Fluoroscopy — steepest percentage decline: 5-month 748 vs 976 (−23.4%); May 164 vs 245 (−33%). Confirm the known scope change before treating as a true demand loss.
  • ⚠️DEXA structural decline: 5-month 1,016 vs 1,318 (−22.9%); May 195 vs 351 (−44%). Endocrinology and Orthopaedic referral pipelines need sustained attention.
  • 📉May softening to watch: Total MID −5.7% vs May 2025, with PET-CT (May 78 vs 109, −28%) and Nuclear Medicine (May 92 vs 134, −31%) both down. Monitor June to separate a seasonal dip from an emerging trend.

🟢 Laboratory — Growth & Strengths (Jan–May)

  • 🥇April and May both elevated: April record 208,677, May second strongest 201,821 — each well above the Q1 average (~174,000/month). Demand stepped up to a higher plateau in Q2.
  • 🧪Chemistry — record April, strong May: April 160,925 (+16.1% YoY), May 154,123. 5-month total 708,900 — 76.1% of all lab volume.
  • 📊5-Month Lab Total: 931,993 tests. Jan–Apr was +7.3% vs 2025; every comparable month in 2026 exceeded its 2025 counterpart (May 2025 not in source). Consistent structural demand increase.
  • 📈Endocrinology sustained high: April 10,738, May 10,276 — both far above the Q1 run-rate (~7,200). 5-month 43,621. Elevated hormone testing has held.
  • 🧬Immunology step-up: April 1,613, May 1,638 (highest of 2026) vs a Q1 run-rate near 1,070. A clear structural increase, not a one-month spike.

🔴 Laboratory — Concerns & Watch Items (Jan–May)

  • ⚠️Molecular below prior year: Jan 2,444 → Feb 2,294 → Mar 1,295 → Apr 1,474 → May 1,752. May recovered but the Jan–Apr base is ~18% under 2025. MSR_2110_02146 (Molecular Technologist) should be filled to stabilise capacity.
  • ⚠️POC well under 2025: Jan 11,098 → Feb 9,807 → Mar 6,348 → Apr 8,308 → May 8,832. Recovering month over month but Jan–Apr is ~20% below the equivalent 2025 period. ER and urgent care utilisation patterns should be reviewed.
  • 📉Blood Bank softening: May 1,144 is the lowest month of 2026 (Jan 1,238 → May 1,144). Inpatient surgical volumes or blood product ordering patterns may have shifted.
  • 📉Satellite clinic divergence (Jan–Apr basis): MSC and DHC trended down while NSC and ABC outperformed. Satellite lab detail was not refreshed for May in this source; carry forward for the next cycle.

🎯 Top 6 Recommended Actions — Based on Jan–May 2026 Complete Data

  • 1️⃣Protect & Grow MRI Momentum: MRI set a record in May (1,524, +20% YoY) and leads MID at +20.1% YTD. Assess additional slot capacity, extended hours, and protocol optimisation so MRI remains the strategic imaging growth driver through Q2–Q3 2026.
  • 2️⃣X-Ray Referral Recovery Initiative: X-Ray is the largest absolute YoY gap (−1,446 studies, −7.3% YTD; −11.1% in May). As ~35% of imaging volume, prioritise a referral source audit and scheduling accessibility review.
  • 3️⃣Validate Fluoroscopy & DEXA Declines: Both are down ~23% YTD. Confirm how much of Fluoroscopy is explained by the known scope change before treating it as lost demand, and review Endocrine/Ortho pathways driving DEXA.
  • 4️⃣Watch the May Normalisation: Both divisions stepped down from the April peak (MID −5.7% YoY; Lab −3.3% MoM). Track June weekly to distinguish a seasonal dip from an emerging downward trend, with particular attention to PET-CT and Nuclear Medicine.
  • 5️⃣Stabilise Molecular & POC Capacity: Both sit well below the 2025 Jan–Apr base (~18% and ~20%). Fill MSR_2110_02146 (Molecular Technologist) and review ER/urgent care POC utilisation to lock in the May recovery.
  • 6️⃣Science Park Scale-Up: SP MID is sustained at roughly 1,700 studies/month (~15% of MID). Evaluate staffing, equipment, and scheduling capacity at SP to capture this elevated baseline.

📈 Multi-Year Growth Context — AHD Diagnostic Center

  • 📌2023 Imaging: 85,766 studies — Baseline year.
  • 📌2024 Imaging: 106,738 studies (+24.4%) — Exceptional expansion. MRI +28.7%, CT +19.8%, US +25.0%.
  • 📌2025 Imaging: 115,833 studies (+8.5%) — Sustainable growth. IR accelerated (+30.7%), PET-CT +17.1%.
  • 📌Jan–May 2026 Imaging: 52,909 studies (−1.1% vs Jan–May 2025 at 53,485): April peaked (11,475) and reconciled US/X-Ray actuals show a broadly flat Ultrasound; May normalised to 11,163. MRI (+20.1% YTD) and CT (+7.4%) carry the division while X-Ray, Fluoroscopy and DEXA drag.
  • 📌Jan–May 2026 Lab: 931,993 tests: Jan–Apr was +7.3% vs 2025 (May 2025 not in source). Growth is broad-based; April (208,677) and May (201,821) are the two strongest months of 2026, indicating a higher demand plateau.
  • 🔑Key Takeaway: Lab demand has stepped up to a higher Q2 plateau, while imaging is roughly flat to slightly down year over year and now softening in May. Priority: sustain MRI and Chemistry, close the X-Ray gap and validate the Fluoroscopy/DEXA declines, stabilise Molecular and POC capacity, and watch whether the May step-down extends into June.
